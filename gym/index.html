CaliCoach

Calisthenics form coach

Start a workout

Analyse a video you recorded (side view works best). It plays at half speed for accuracy.

Plays generated workouts (no camera) so you can see how recognition, rep counting and form feedback work.

Set up your phone
  • Prop the phone 2–3 m away so your whole body is in the picture, head to feet, through the whole movement.
  • Side view for push-ups, squats, dips, rows, lunges and leg raises. Front view works for pull-ups and shows knees caving in on squats.
  • Good light, nothing behind you that looks like a person, and fitted clothes help the tracking.
  • Keep the phone upright and level; the app uses gravity to judge body lines.
